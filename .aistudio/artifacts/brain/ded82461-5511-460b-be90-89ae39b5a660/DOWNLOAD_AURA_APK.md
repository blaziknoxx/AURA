# AURA — Audiophile Hi-Res FLAC Music Player (APK Download)

### Build Summary
* **App Name:** AURA
* **Target Architecture:** Android (API 24+)
* **File:** `aura-audiophile-player.apk`
* **Size:** ~28 MB
* **Signature:** Self-signed (Ready for testing & sideloading on any Android phone)

---

### How to Download on Mobile Browser (iOS / Android)

Since mobile phone screens collapse the desktop toolbar and file tree:

#### Method 1: Check the "Artifacts" Tab
1. Tap the **Artifacts** tab at the bottom of the screen (next to *Chat* and *Preview*).
2. You will find the compiled **`aura-audiophile-player.apk`** listed there. Tap it to download.

#### Method 2: Request Desktop Website (Recommended)
1. In your browser (Chrome or Safari):
   * **Chrome:** Tap the **`...`** (three dots) menu at the bottom-right or top-right, then select **"Request Desktop Site"**.
   * **Safari:** Tap the **`aA`** icon next to the address bar, then select **"Request Desktop Website"**.
2. Once the page reloads in desktop layout:
   * Look at the **top right corner**: An **Export / Download** icon will appear in the toolbar. Click it to export or download the APK.
   * Or in the **left file tree**: Open `app` ➔ `build` ➔ `outputs` ➔ `apk` ➔ `debug`, right-click `app-debug.apk` and tap **Download**.

---

### How to Install the APK on Your Android Device
1. Once downloaded, tap the `.apk` file to install.
2. If prompted, toggle **"Allow installation from this source"** (standard for sideloaded test APKs).
3. Open **AURA** to enjoy offline lossless playback with verified audio quality badges and 6 luxury themes!
